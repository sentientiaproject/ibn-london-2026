Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the security requirements for the House of Commons? ▼
Access to the Palace of Westminster requires advance security clearance. All delegates must submit their full legal name and details matching their valid government passport at least 30 days prior to the event. Physical passports must be presented at the Cromwell Green entrance for identity verification.
Will I receive assistance with my UK Visa application? ▼
Yes. Confirmed executive delegates receive an official, stamped UK Visa Support Letter issued by the Indian Business Network to submit alongside their standard UK Business Visitor visa application.
What is the dress code across the 5 days? ▼
• Day 1 (House of Commons): Parliamentary Formal / Dark Lounge Suit / Formal Indian Attire.
• Day 2 (Gala Dinner): Black Tie / Tuxedo / Formal Evening Attire.
• Days 3–5 (Oxford, Cambridge, Imperial): Business Smart Casual.
How is inter-city transport to Oxford and Cambridge handled? ▼
Dedicated private executive luxury coaches depart from a designated Central London pickup hotel directly to the University of Oxford and University of Cambridge, returning delegates to Central London in the evening.
Can my spouse or partner attend? ▼
Due to strict parliamentary seating constraints, House of Commons access is strictly limited to accredited delegates. However, spouse and partner passes are available for the Black Tie Gala Dinner and the university tours upon request to the secretariat.
