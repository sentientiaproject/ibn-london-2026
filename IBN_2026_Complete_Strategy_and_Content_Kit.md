- **App Name:** `IBN London 2026` / `IBN Global Leaders`
- **Framework:** Flutter / React Native with Native iOS & Android bridge.
- **Target OS:** iOS 16+ & Android 12+
- **Security & Access Control:** Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) — Public Mode vs. Verified Delegate Mode (Authentication via Delegate ID / One-Time Passcode).

### 2. Core Functional Modules

#### A. Digital Delegate Pass & Security Clearance
- Encrypted Dynamic QR Code for daily credential verification and venue entry.
- Parliament Security Status tracker (Pending, Clearance Granted, Badge Issued).
- Emergency digital pass backup and offline wallet integration (Apple Wallet & Google Wallet pass support).

#### B. Dynamic 5-Day Interactive Itinerary
- Real-time schedule updates for House of Commons, Black Tie Gala, Oxford, Cambridge, and Imperial College London.
- Integrated GPS navigation and group transport pickup timers (Central London luxury coaches to Oxford & Cambridge).
- Add-to-calendar sync (Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Outlook).
- Dress Code indicators per session (e.g., Parliamentary Formal / Business Formal for Nov 5; Black Tie / National Formal for Nov 6; Smart Casual / Academic for Nov 7-9).

#### C. Private C-Suite Networking Directory
- Searchable directory of verified delegates (Name, Designation, Company, Sector, Country, LinkedIn URL).
- 1-on-1 In-App Meeting Scheduler: Request and confirm 15-minute networking catch-ups during designated breaks.
- Secure direct messaging (delegate privacy toggle: delegates can hide contact numbers while messaging inside the app).
- Digital Business Card Exchange (Instant NFC or QR scan).

#### D. Live Event Stream & Push Notifications
- Push notification system for real-time security alerts (e.g., "Parliament Security: Please arrive at Cromwell Green entrance by 13:45 with your passport").
- Interactive Q&A and polling module during the Oxford Business Leadership Forum.
- Session reminders 30 minutes before each departure and event commencement.

#### E. Media & High-Resolution Photography Portal
- Real-time professional photographer photo sync categorized by day:
  - Day 1: House of Commons Awards & Reception
  - Day 2: Black Tie Gala Dinner
  - Day 3: Oxford Forum

#### F. Executive Concierge & London City Guide
- Partner luxury hotels near Westminster and Mayfair.
- Chauffeur and shuttle bus coordination.
- Visa guidance and embassy liaison contact.
- Direct contact with Event Coordinator (Subha Austalekshmi) via in-app WhatsApp / hotline trigger.
